What happens when chess and Korean chess fight on the same board?
Chess vs Janggi pits a chess army against a janggi (Korean chess) army on one janggi board.

■ Two armies, two sets of rules
Chess pieces move by chess rules. Janggi pieces move by janggi rules. Checkmate the enemy king or general to win.
- Chess: a queen and three bishops, a king that roams the whole board, pawns that promote
- Janggi: a horde of nine soldiers and five chariots, cannons that jump over any piece, a general guarded inside the palace

■ Pick your side
- vs Computer: play as Chess or Janggi against Easy, Normal or Hard
- Online: create a room and send the 6-character code to a friend. The host picks the side
- Undo

■ New to one of the games? No problem
- One-page rules with how every piece of both armies moves
- Legal moves shown right on the board
- A clear warning whenever you are in check

■ Rules of this game
- Pieces sit on the points of a janggi board, and Chess moves first
- Castling and the pawn's double step are in. There is no en passant
- There is no facing-generals rule and no passing
- Draw by stalemate, threefold repetition, or fifty moves without a capture or pawn move
